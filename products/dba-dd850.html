Overview

Largest of the DD range. 850 CMH process airflow with three-phase power for industrial low-temperature operations.

The DBA-DD850 is part of the Desiccant Rotary family — 'ProFlute' desiccant rotor with PTC ceramic reactivation heater. Operates from −20°C to 40°C, holds 1–90% RH — the choice for cold rooms, lithium battery production, and pharmaceuticals.

It is engineered, tested, and manufactured at DBA Electric Pte. Ltd., a Singapore-headquartered humidity-control specialist with over twenty years of design and production experience. Every unit ships with international certifications (CE, CB, ISO 9001, RoHS) and is supported by a 12-month manufacturer warranty.

Ervee handles international distribution: dealer agreements, OEM/ODM partnerships, container-load shipping, and direct enterprise inquiries from outside the Singapore and Hong Kong domestic markets.

How does DBA-DD850 compare to other models in the Desiccant Rotary range?

DBA-DD850 is positioned at 7.5 kg/hr capacity within the Desiccant Rotary family that spans 1 kg/hr to 7.5 kg/hr. Use the comparison table above to evaluate against the other models in the same series.

What is included in the standard package?

Standard packaging includes the DBA-DD850 unit, mounting hardware, drainage hose, power cable, control panel with cable, remote controller (where applicable), and operating manual. Specific in-the-box contents are listed in the configuration table on this page.

What certifications does DBA-DD850 carry?

All DBA dehumidifiers carry CE marking for European market access, the CB scheme for international electrical-safety reciprocity, ISO 9001 quality certification, and RoHS compliance.

What warranty applies?

Standard manufacturer warranty is 12 months from invoice date for parts and labour. Extended warranty options are available through dealer networks. Spare parts are stocked in Singapore for 7+ years from model phase-out.

Is DBA-DD850 available for OEM/ODM programmes?

Yes. Available customisations include voltage variants (110V 60Hz, 220-240V 50Hz, 380V 3-phase), plug types, label and packaging changes, custom enclosure colours, and (for sufficient volumes) bespoke control firmware. Submit project details via the contact form.

How does shipping work for international orders?

Ervee handles FCL container loads and LCL part-shipments from Singapore and Hong Kong worldwide. Standard models in stock typically ship within 5-10 business days; made-to-order configurations take 4-6 weeks; bespoke OEM/ODM with custom firmware takes 8-12 weeks for first runs.
