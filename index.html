Technology

Three principles.
Every climate solved.

Choosing the right dehumidifier starts with understanding how it removes water from air. Each method excels in different conditions.

01

Refrigerant (compressor)

The workhorse for tropical and temperate climates. Air is drawn over a cold evaporator coil — moisture condenses, falls into a collection system, and dry air is returned to the room.

  • Operates 5 – 40°C
  • Highest extraction per kWh in warm, humid air
  • Used across UTC, GEC, GE, GEX, and DH product lines
02

Desiccant (rotary)

A silica honeycomb wheel actively adsorbs water vapour — even at sub-zero temperatures where compressors stall. Continuous, precise, low-temperature drying.

  • Operates from −20°C to 40°C
  • Holds RH 1–90% with high precision
  • Cold storage, pharma, lithium battery rooms, lyophilisation
03

Humidity Control Unit

Hybrid units that hold a target RH band by removing or adding moisture as needed. Wet-membrane evaporative humidification, isenthalpic and clean.

  • Two-way control around a setpoint
  • UV-C sterilisation and RS485 BMS integration
  • Galleries, museums, wine cellars, data centres

Applications

Built for every environment.

DBA dehumidifiers are deployed across six continents in markets ranging from luxury residences to heavy industry.

⌂

Residential & Hospitality

Apartments, villas, hotels, and short-stay rentals. Quiet ceiling and mobile units suit both retrofit and new-build projects.

▢

Commercial & Office

Retail, offices, gyms, and F&B venues. Concealed ceiling-mount units distribute dry air through ductwork to multiple zones.

⬛

Industrial & Logistics

Warehouses, manufacturing, switchgear rooms. Three-phase floor units with stainless tanks and 10 m drainage head.

◈

Heritage & Art

Museums, galleries, archives, and wine cellars. HC humidistats hold tight RH bands for collections and aging conditions.

❄

Cold & Pharma

Cold storage, pharmaceutical packing, lithium battery production. Desiccant rotary units operate down to −20°C.

⬢

Data & Telecom

Server rooms, switching cabinets, telecom shelters. RS485 / Modbus units integrate with BMS, with redundant alarming.

⌬

Restoration & Recovery

Flood recovery, post-leak drying, construction site dry-out. Portable LGR units roll in and start drying immediately.

⌗

Specialty & Custom

Indoor pools, aquatic centres, music studios, cigar lounges. OEM/ODM solutions available for branded distribution.
