The DBA difference

Why specifiers, buyers, and dealers choose DBA.

Three reasons the catalogue keeps growing in international markets — and why the DBA name keeps appearing in commercial specifications, restoration fleets, and architect-led residential projects.

01

Engineering depth, not just product count.

Many catalogues offer one or two dehumidifier types. DBA covers the full thermodynamic spectrum: refrigerant compressor for tropical climates, low-grain refrigerant (LGR) for restoration, two-way humidity control for galleries and data centres, and desiccant rotary for cold-room and pharmaceutical environments.

Every model is engineered in-house. Every spec is published. Every catalogue includes complete technical data — process parameters, electrical specifications, dimensional drawings, configuration tables, and control matrices.

02

Built for international compliance.

Every unit ships with CE marking for European market access, the CB scheme for global electrical-safety reciprocity, RoHS compliance, and ISO 9001 quality-management certification. Selected residential models additionally carry Hong Kong Grade 1 Energy Label.

Voltage variants are available across the range — 220-240V single-phase 50Hz for most regions, 380V three-phase 50Hz for industrial models, 110V 60Hz for North America on request. Plug types and labels customised per shipment.

03

Long-term reliability and lifecycle support.

A DBA dehumidifier is engineered for a long service life. Inverter compressors, metal-cast motors with Japan NSK bearings, multi-stage shock-absorbing mounts, and cold-rolled steel enclosures resist the wear that ages cheaper units within a few years. Field experience across the regional network shows units running continuously for a decade with only routine filter and pump-cartridge maintenance.

Lifecycle support is built into the operating model. Every commercial and industrial model integrates fault detection, water-leak sensor ports, and BMS RS485 reporting — so problems are caught before they become outages. Spare parts are stocked in Singapore for at least seven years from the date a model is phased out, and engineering support remains available direct from the factory throughout the product's life.

Buyer's guide

Choosing an industrial dehumidifier: a practical guide.

Specifiers, facilities managers, and dealers face the same question: which dehumidifier is right for this space? The answer depends on five variables — temperature, humidity load, target RH, airflow constraints, and integration requirements. This guide explains each.

1. Start with the temperature range.

The single biggest determinant of dehumidifier type is operating temperature. Refrigerant (compressor) units operate efficiently between 5°C and 40°C. They are by far the most cost-effective choice for residential, commercial, and most industrial environments in tropical and temperate climates.

Below 5°C, refrigerant coils begin to frost and dehumidification efficiency collapses. For cold storage, refrigerated warehouses, pharmaceutical packing rooms, and lithium-battery production facilities, the answer is a desiccant rotary dehumidifier that operates down to −20°C using a silica honeycomb wheel.

2. Calculate the moisture load.

For typical residential and small commercial environments, the rule of thumb is 0.5 to 1 litre per day per 10 sq ft of floor area to maintain 50-60% RH. Heavy-load environments — basements, indoor pools, post-flood drying scenes, indoor agriculture — demand 5 to 10 times that capacity.

For industrial spaces with continuous moisture infiltration (warehouses, switchgear rooms, manufacturing), the calculation includes air changes per hour, infiltration rates, and process moisture release. A 5,000 sq ft industrial warehouse in tropical conditions typically requires a 400-550 L/day floor-standing unit such as the DBA-GE400LD-HP or GE550LD-HP.

3. Decide on the target relative humidity.

Comfort and mould prevention require 50-60% RH. Below 40%, the air feels uncomfortably dry and certain materials (wood, leather, paper) begin to crack. Above 65%, condensation, mould, and dust mite proliferation become real risks.

Specialist environments target tighter bands. Galleries, museums, and archives typically maintain 50-55% RH within ±2% to preserve organic collections. Wine cellars hold 60-70% RH for cork integrity. Data centres sit in the 40-60% band per ASHRAE TC 9.9 guidelines, with tight upper limits to prevent condensation on cold equipment surfaces.

4. Consider airflow, ductwork, and noise.

Ceiling-mounted units distribute dry air through ductwork to multiple rooms — quieter and more even than placing portable units in each space. Slim-line UTC models install in just 200-310 mm of plenum height, making them suitable for retrofit projects with limited ceiling void.

Static pressure ratings matter. The DBA UTC range delivers 80-100 Pa of static pressure, sufficient for short residential duct runs. For longer commercial duct distributions, the GEC series provides 80-100 Pa with 1,200-3,250 CMH airflow, enough to serve multiple zones from a single chassis.

5. Plan integration and control.

Standalone control via touch panel and remote is fine for residential. Commercial and industrial deployments require integration with the building management system. Every UTC, GEC, GE, HC, and DD model includes RS485 with Modbus protocol — direct BMS integration with centralised setpoint control, alarming, and runtime telemetry.

For consumer-facing applications, smaller UTC and GEC68 models also offer WiFi App control, allowing end-users to adjust setpoint and view current humidity from their phone. The mobile app is published in major regional app stores.

Putting it together: a sample specification.

A 2,500 sq ft luxury condominium in Singapore with ducted ceiling distribution, target 55% RH, BMS integration, and quiet operation: specify DBA-UTC120 (120 L/day, 50 dB(A), WiFi + RS485, 310 mm slim chassis). Two units handle the load with redundancy.

A 4,000 sq ft pharmaceutical packing room operating at 18°C with target 30% RH: specify DBA-DD550 (2.5 kg/hr capacity, −20°C operating envelope, 7-inch LCD with humidity datalog, BMS RS485). One unit handles the load; a second is recommended for redundancy in critical applications.

A restoration crew responding to flood damage in 2,000 sq ft commercial properties: specify DBA-GEX110 portable LGR units (110 L/day, R290 refrigerant, stackable for fleet transport, WiFi diagnostics for crew supervision).

Common questions

Answers for buyers and dealers.

What is DBA Electric Pte. Ltd. and how does it relate to Ervee?

DBA Electric Pte. Ltd. is a Singapore-headquartered manufacturer of humidity-control equipment with over twenty years of design and manufacturing experience. Ervee is the international distribution and showcase brand for DBA — handling export sales, dealer agreements, project specifications, and direct enterprise inquiries from outside the Singapore and Hong Kong domestic markets. Both brands are part of the same group.

Where do shipments originate?

FCL and LCL shipments leave from Singapore (Port of Singapore) and Hong Kong (Kwai Tsing Container Terminals). Ervee handles full export documentation, certificate of origin, and CE/CB compliance paperwork as standard. Smaller volumes can also be air-freighted for time-critical deployments.

What is the typical lead time?

Standard models in stock ship within 5-10 business days. Made-to-order configurations (regional voltage variants, alternative plug types) typically take 4-6 weeks from PO. Container-scale orders with project-specific configuration take 8-12 weeks for the first run, with shorter cycles thereafter.

What are the minimum order quantities?

For standard catalogue models, the MOQ is one unit for sample orders, with project-volume discounts kicking in at 10, 50, and container-load (200+) units. Commercial procurement contacts should reach out via the form below for the current tier pricing relevant to their volume and territory.

Are payment terms negotiable?

Initial orders run on 30% deposit T/T with balance against B/L copy or letter of credit at sight. Established dealer accounts move to net 30 or net 60 against credit-checked terms. Container-load orders may qualify for documentary collection or open account based on relationship history.

What technical support is provided?

All dealers receive product training (factory or remote), sizing tools and calculators, marketing collateral (catalogues, datasheets, video), and direct engineering support from the Singapore factory. Service training and spare-parts kits are available for in-region after-sales programmes.

How does an exclusive territory agreement work?

Region-locked dealer agreements are available for committed partners with proven distribution capability. Exclusive territories require a minimum annual purchase commitment, a documented go-to-market plan, evidence of regional distribution coverage, and a clear after-sales / service strategy. Initial conversations begin via the partnership form on this page.

What regional adaptations are available?

Yes. Standard regional adaptations include voltage variants (110V 60Hz for North America, 220-240V 50Hz for most of the world, 380V 3-phase for industrial models), plug types (Type A/B/C/G/I depending on region), packaging adjustments, and English-language manuals matched to your market.
