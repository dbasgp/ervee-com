Buyer's guide

Choosing an industrial dehumidifier: a practical guide.

Five variables decide which dehumidifier fits your space: temperature, humidity load, target RH, airflow, and integration.

1. Start with the temperature range.

Refrigerant (compressor) units run efficiently from 5°C to 40°C — the right choice for residential, commercial, and most industrial environments in tropical and temperate climates.

Below 5°C, refrigerant coils frost and efficiency collapses. For cold storage, refrigerated warehouses, pharma packing rooms, and lithium-battery production, choose a desiccant rotary unit operating down to −20°C.

2. Calculate the moisture load.

Residential rule of thumb: 0.5–1 L/day per 10 sq ft to hold 50–60% RH. Heavy loads (basements, indoor pools, flood drying) demand 5–10× that.

Industrial spaces factor in air changes per hour, infiltration, and process moisture. A 5,000 sq ft tropical warehouse typically needs a 400–550 L/day unit (DBA-GE400LD-HP or GE550LD-HP).

3. Decide on the target relative humidity.

50–60% RH is the comfort and mould-prevention sweet spot. Below 40% materials crack; above 65% mould and dust mites proliferate.

Specialist targets: galleries and archives 50–55% ±2%; wine cellars 60–70%; data centres 40–60% per ASHRAE TC 9.9.

4. Consider airflow, ductwork, and noise.

Ceiling units distribute dry air through ductwork to multiple rooms — quieter and more even than portable units. Slim UTC models install in 200–310 mm of plenum.

Static pressure matters: UTC delivers 80–100 Pa for residential duct runs; GEC handles longer commercial distributions at 1,200–3,250 CMH from a single chassis.

5. Plan integration and control.

Touch panel and remote work for residential. Commercial and industrial deployments need BMS integration — every UTC, GEC, GE, HC, and DD model includes RS485 Modbus for centralised setpoint, alarming, and telemetry.

Smaller UTC and GEC68 models also offer WiFi App control for end-users.

Three sample specifications.

A 2,500 sq ft condominium with ducted distribution, 55% RH target, BMS integration: specify DBA-UTC120 (120 L/day, 50 dB(A), WiFi + RS485). Two units for redundancy.

A 4,000 sq ft pharma packing room at 18°C, 30% RH target: specify DBA-DD550 (2.5 kg/hr, −20°C envelope, 7-inch LCD). One handles the load; a second for critical-application redundancy.

A restoration crew handling 2,000 sq ft flood scenes: specify DBA-GEX110 (110 L/day LGR, R290 refrigerant, stackable, WiFi diagnostics).

Common questions

Quick answers.

What dehumidifier products are available?

A complete portfolio engineered for every climate — from 20 L/day residential ceiling units to 7.5 kg/hr industrial desiccants. Always in stock for fast worldwide shipping.

What is the difference between refrigerant and desiccant dehumidifiers?

Refrigerant units cool incoming air past its dew point so moisture condenses out — most efficient in warm conditions (5–40°C). Desiccant units use a silica wheel to adsorb water vapour directly and operate down to −20°C, making them the right choice for cold storage, pharmaceuticals, and lithium battery production.

What certifications are included?

CE for European markets, CB scheme for international electrical-safety reciprocity, ISO 9001 quality management, and RoHS compliance. Selected residential models also carry Hong Kong Grade 1 Energy Label.

How do I size a unit for my space?

The buyer's guide above outlines the basic approach. For exact sizing, share floor area, ceiling height, target RH, and any special conditions (indoor pool, cold room, etc.) via the contact form — we'll model it and recommend a configuration.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. Container-load (FCL) and less-than-container (LCL) shipments worldwide. Smaller volumes can also be air-freighted for time-critical projects.

Are dealer partnerships available?
